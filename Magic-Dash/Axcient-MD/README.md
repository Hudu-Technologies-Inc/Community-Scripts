Axcient Magic Dash
==================

See Axcient x360Recover backup health on your company pages in Hudu.

Before you start
----------------

Have your Hudu web address, Hudu API key, and Axcient API key ready. Use a
Windows computer with Git for Windows installed and access to GitHub. On
first launch, the app may download PowerShell if it needs a newer version.

This test build works with the Hudu test site set up for your Windows account.
If the app says the site has not been configured, contact the person who sent
you the app.

Run your first sync
-------------------

1. Double-click Axcient-Magic-Dash.exe. Enter your Hudu web address and both
   API keys, then click Connect. Select Remember me if you want the app to
   save the connection on this computer.
2. Pick a Hudu company on the left and its matching Axcient client on the
   right. Click Match selected. Repeat for each company you want to sync.
3. Click Sync now and confirm. Check the result at the top of the app. In Hudu,
   open a matched company and look for the Axcient X360Recover tile on its
   Overview page.

How to use
----------

Connect or change your sign-in

- Connect checks your Hudu and Axcient details and opens the company lists.
- Remember me saves the connection on this computer for the next time you
  open the app. Automatic sync needs this setting.
- Connection settings takes you back to the sign-in screen. Edit your
  details there and click Connect again.

Match or unmatch a company

- Match selected links the Hudu company you picked on the left to the
  Axcient client you picked on the right. The pair is saved for future syncs.
- Unmatch selected removes a saved pair. Select either member of the pair,
  click Unmatch selected, then pick the correct company and client if you
  want to match them again. To remove a tile already in Hudu, use the tile
  cleanup steps below.

Run a sync and show devices in Hudu

- Sync now updates the backup health tile for each matched company. Click
  it, confirm, and check the results at the top of the app.
- Load layouts shows the device layouts available in Hudu. Pick one and
  click Repair and bind to use it and add any missing Axcient fields.
- Create and bind makes a new layout and selects it for sync. Enter a name
  such as Axcient Devices before clicking. Once a layout is bound, the next
  sync adds or updates individual device records in that layout.
- Archive retired managed device assets archives app-managed device
  records for devices that are no longer in the matched Axcient inventory.
  Select it before clicking Sync now if you want to archive those records.

Set up or stop automatic sync

1. Connect with Remember me selected.
2. Enter an interval from 1 to 48 hours. For example, enter 8 to sync every
   eight hours. Click Save schedule to start or update the schedule.
3. Click Check schedule to see whether it's active and when it will run
   next. Automatic sync runs while you're signed in to Windows.
4. To stop automatic sync, click Disable and confirm. You can still use
   Sync now whenever you need it.

If you move or rename the app, open it from its new location and click
Save schedule again.

Remove a Magic Dash tile

1. Click Scan tiles to list Magic Dash tiles across Hudu.
2. Pick the tile from the list and check its company and title.
3. Click Delete selected tile and confirm. The app removes that tile
   from Hudu. A future sync can recreate an Axcient tile for a company
   that is still matched.
